Growing Food. Creating Wealth. Transforming Communities.

AGROFARMS Co-operative Society Ltd. is an integrated agricultural enterprise committed to transforming agriculture in South Sudan through sustainable farming, modern production techniques and innovative agribusiness solutions. Our activities focus on crop production, livestock rearing, poultry farming, freshwater aquaculture, processing and value addition.

WHO WE ARE

AGROFARMS CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETY LTD. (ACS) is a private agricultural enterprise established to develop a modern, commercially viable and sustainable mixed farming operation in the Republic of South Sudan.

The company was established to address the growing demand for quality food products while contributing to food security, employment creation, rural development and economic diversification.

AGROFARMS operates across Tonj Payam, Pawel Payam and Manyang-ngok Payam in Tonj South County, Warrap State, covering approximately 150–200 hectares of agricultural land.

Our integrated farming model combines crop production, livestock rearing, poultry farming, freshwater aquaculture, agro-processing and value addition.

What we do

AGROFARMS Co-operative Society Ltd. is engaged in a wide range of integrated agricultural and agribusiness activities aimed at increasing food production, promoting sustainable farming and strengthening the agricultural value chain in South Sudan.

Commercial Crop Production

We cultivate food and cash crops on a commercial scale using modern and sustainable farming practices to improve productivity and meet market demand.

Livestock Breeding and Ranching

We engage in livestock breeding and commercial ranching to support the production of quality meat, milk and other animal products.

Poultry Production

We undertake poultry farming for the production of eggs and poultry meat to support food supply and meet growing consumer demand.

Freshwater Fish Farming

We develop aquaculture activities to increase freshwater fish production, improve food availability and promote sustainable fish farming.

Seed Production and Multiplication

We focus on producing and multiplying quality seeds to support farmers, improve crop yields and strengthen agricultural productivity.

Agro-processing and Value Addition

We process agricultural products into value-added goods to improve product quality, reduce post-harvest losses and increase market value.

Food Packaging and Branding

We package and brand agricultural products to enhance product presentation, recognition, quality and competitiveness in the market.

Agricultural Storage and Logistics

We focus on proper storage, handling and transportation of agricultural products to support efficient distribution and minimise post-harvest losses.

Wholesale and Retail Marketing

We market and distribute agricultural products through wholesale and retail channels, connecting producers with consumers and other markets.

Agricultural Training and Extension

We support farmers through agricultural training, extension services and capacity development to promote modern farming knowledge and practical skills.

Agricultural Inputs and Farm Services

We provide agricultural inputs and related farm services to support farming operations and improve access to essential agricultural resources.
